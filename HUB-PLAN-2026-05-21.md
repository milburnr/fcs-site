# Hub Plan — Three Missing Parent Hubs

- **Site:** floridaconstructionspecialists.com
- **Source:** site audit 2026-05-19 (`content-ops/tools/site-audit/runs/fcs/2026-05-19/audit.md` §4)
- **Decision date:** 2026-05-21 (Rich asked for a detailed plan; this is it)

## What we're fixing

Three service families have 8 city spokes each but no parent hub:

| Family | Existing spokes | Parent hub |
|---|---|---|
| `commercial-construction-<city>/` | Tampa, Bradenton, Brandon, Clearwater, Lakeland, Ruskin, Sarasota, St. Petersburg (8) | **missing** |
| `condo-remediation-<city>/` | same 8 cities | **missing** |
| `insurance-restoration-<city>/` | same 8 cities | **missing** |

Six other service families (balcony-reconstruction, disaster-recovery, exterior-waterproofing, historic-restoration, luxury-custom-homes, multi-family-construction) already have working parent hubs at `/<service>/`. The pattern we're applying is proven on those.

## What "good" looks like — the model hub

The working model is `/balcony-reconstruction/`. Its on-page structure (sampled 2026-05-21):

1. H1 hero — "Balcony Reconstruction Tampa & Florida Gulf Coast"
2. H2 — "Tampa Bay's Trusted Balcony Reconstruction Contractor" (intro / authority)
3. H2 — "Why Balconies Fail in Florida's Climate" (problem framing)
4. H2 — "Signs Your Balcony Needs Professional Attention" (intent capture)
5. H2 — "Our Comprehensive Balcony Assessment Process" (FCS process)
6. H2 — "Balcony Reconstruction Methods and Techniques" (technical depth)
7. H2 — "Balcony Waterproofing Systems for Florida's Climate" (specialty depth)
8. H2 — "Condominium and HOA Balcony Projects" (audience-specific)
9. FAQ block (visible DOM only — no FAQPage JSON-LD per Phase 2 of AUDIT-FIXES)
10. CTA — "Start Your Project"
11. Related Reading + **8 city-spoke links**

That's the shape to clone for the three new hubs. Use the regional anchor "Tampa Bay" (not any one city) and link out to all 8 city spokes prominently.
